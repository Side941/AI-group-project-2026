==============================================================================
PART 4 — SUMMARY FOR THE REPORT
==============================================================================
- The pool contains 4 chunks mentioning suicid*; BM25 can reach them only via exact lexical overlap.
- 3/10 suicidal-class dev posts retrieve at least one such chunk in the top-5 — the lexical bridge the BM25 choice relies on.
- The class-characteristic terms table (Part 2) shows the signal terms
  are rare in the pool (high IDF), i.e. when they match, they dominate
  ranking — the regime where sparse lexical retrieval is strongest and
  a dense encoder adds least for the routing decision.
- Caveat to state honestly: this justifies BM25 for posts with explicit
  lexical markers. Posts expressing ideation only implicitly (no shared
  vocabulary) are the residual case for dense/hybrid retrieval — cf.
  the per-class hit rates above and RQ3 error analysis.
